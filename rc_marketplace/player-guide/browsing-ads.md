# Browsing Ads

## Home screen

The home screen has two sections:

- **Featured carousel** — promoted ads in a horizontal slider at the top.
- **Category buttons** — tap any category to jump to its listings: Cars, Items, Jobs, Real Estates, Weapons, Other.

## Category listings

Each category page shows active ads for that category, newest first. Each listing card shows the title, price, location label, a thumbnail, and the like count.

The listing page has a search bar and sort/filter controls:

- **Search** — filters by title text.
- **Sort** — Newest, Oldest, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low.
- **Price range filter** — set a Min and a Max to narrow results.

Click **Read More** on a card to open the full ad view.

## Full ad view

The full ad view shows:

- All images in a swipeable carousel
- Title, price, description, and location label
- Category-specific details (mileage and tuning for cars; salary and job title for jobs; property type and house number for real estates; weapon type, condition, and license requirement for weapons; amount for items and other)
- **Map widget** — if the poster set a map pin, the GTA V map shows the pinned location. A waypoint button in the map corner sets a route on your minimap. See [Setting a Waypoint](/rc_marketplace/player-guide/setting-a-waypoint).
- **Like button** — toggles the ad in your saved list and updates the public like count.
- **Contact seller** button — opens a direct message thread with the ad owner. See [Messaging Sellers](/rc_marketplace/player-guide/messaging-sellers).

## My Ads

The **My Ads** tab shows all ads you have posted. From here you can:

- **Edit** — re-opens the form pre-filled with the existing data. An edit cost is charged on save. Adding the promoted flag to a previously unpromoted ad also charges the promotion cost.
- **Delete** — a confirmation dialog appears before deletion.

## Favorites

The **Favorites** tab (heart icon in the navigation bar) shows every ad you have liked. The list persists across sessions. Use the **Clear All** button to remove all saved ads at once.
