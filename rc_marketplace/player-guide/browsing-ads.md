# Browsing Ads

## Home screen

The home screen has two sections:

- **Featured carousel** — promoted ads in a horizontal slider at the top. Up to 20 promoted ads are shown.
- **Category buttons** — tap any category to jump to its listing grid: Cars, Items, Jobs, Real Estates, Weapons, Other.

## Category listings

Each category page shows active ads for that category, newest first (up to 50 per load). Each listing card shows the title, price, location label, a thumbnail, and the like count.

The listing page has a search bar and sort/filter controls:

- **Search** — filters by title text.
- **Sort** — Newest, Oldest, Price: Low to High, Price: High to Low.
- **Price range filter** — set a Min and Max to narrow results.

Click **Read More** on a card to open the full ad view.

## Full ad view

The full ad view shows:

- All images in a swipeable carousel.
- Title, price, description, location label.
- Category-specific details (mileage and tuning for cars; salary and job title for jobs; property type and house number for real estates; weapon type, condition, and license requirement for weapons; amount for items and other).
- **Map widget** — if the poster set a map pin, a Leaflet map shows the pinned location. A navigation-arrow button in the map corner sets a waypoint on your minimap. See [Setting a Waypoint](/rc_marketplace/player-guide/setting-a-waypoint).
- **Like button** — heart icon. Toggling it adds or removes the ad from your saved list and updates the public like count.
- **Contact seller / Chat with Poster** button — opens a direct message thread with the ad owner. See [Messaging Sellers](/rc_marketplace/player-guide/messaging-sellers).

## My Ads

The **My Ads** tab shows all ads you have posted. From here you can:

- **Edit** — re-opens the creation form pre-filled with existing data. The edit cost is deducted on save. Adding the promoted flag to a previously unpromoted ad also charges the promotion cost.
- **Delete** — a confirmation dialog appears before deletion.

## Saved ads (Favorites)

The **Favorites** tab (heart icon in the navigation bar) shows every ad you have liked. This list is tied to your player identifier and persists across sessions. You can clear the entire list with the **Clear All** button.
