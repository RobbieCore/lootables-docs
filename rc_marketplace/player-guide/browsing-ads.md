# Browsing Ads

## Home screen

The home screen has two sections:

- **Featured carousel** — promoted ads shown in a horizontal slider at the top.
- **Category buttons** — tap any category to jump to its listing grid: Cars, Jobs, Items, Real Estates, Weapons, Other.

## Category listings

Each category page shows all active ads for that category, newest first. Each listing card shows the title, price, location label, a thumbnail image, and the like count.

Click **Read More** on a card to open the full ad view.

## Full ad view

The full ad view shows:

- All images (swipeable).
- Title, price, description, location label.
- Category-specific details (mileage and tuning for cars; salary and job title for jobs; etc.).
- **Map widget** — if the poster set a map pin, a Leaflet map shows the pinned location. A navigation-arrow icon button in the map corner sets a waypoint on your minimap. See [Setting a Waypoint](/rc_marketplace/player-guide/setting-a-waypoint).
- **Like button** — heart icon. Toggling it adds/removes the ad from your saved list and increments/decrements the public like count.
- **Chat with Poster** button — opens a direct message thread with the ad owner. See [Messaging Sellers](/rc_marketplace/player-guide/messaging-sellers).

## My Ads

The **My Ads** tab in the navigation bar shows all ads you have posted. From here you can:

- **Edit** an ad — re-opens the creation form prefilled with existing data. The edit cost is deducted on save. Promoting an unpromoted ad also charges the promotion cost.
- **Delete** an ad — a confirmation dialog appears before deletion.

## Saved ads

The heart/bookmark icon in the navigation bar opens your liked ads. This list is local to your account — liking and unliking updates in real time.
